📖 Big Idea 1: Four Hemispheres

A hemisphere is half of Earth. The equator is an imaginary line around Earth's middle. It divides Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres. The prime meridian runs from the North Pole to the South Pole. It divides Earth into the Eastern and Western Hemispheres.
💡 Remember: Equator = 0° latitude. Prime meridian = 0° longitude.

🧭 Big Idea 2: Latitude and Longitude

Latitude lines run east to west (sideways). They measure how far north or south a place is from the equator.
Longitude lines run north to south (top to bottom). They measure how far east or west a place is from the prime meridian.
🎯 These imaginary lines help us find an absolute location, an exact position on Earth.

🏔️ Big Idea 3: America's Mountain Ranges

Appalachian Mountains: Eastern North America, including Georgia. Almost 2,000 miles long; very old, forest-covered mountains.
Rocky Mountains: Western North America. More than 3,000 miles long; includes some of North America's tallest mountains.

🌊 Big Idea 4: Six Important Rivers

Mississippi: Major central U.S. river flowing toward the Gulf of Mexico.
Ohio: Flows west to join the Mississippi River.
Rio Grande: Begins in Colorado and flows to the Gulf of Mexico; forms part of the U.S.–Mexico border.
Colorado: River of the southwestern U.S., famous for the Grand Canyon.
Hudson: Flows through eastern New York into New York Harbor.
St. Lawrence: Connects the Great Lakes to the Atlantic Ocean.
